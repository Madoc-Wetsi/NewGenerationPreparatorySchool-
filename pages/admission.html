Admission

Join the New Generation Preparatory School family

Start Your Child's Journey

At New Generation Preparatory School, we welcome families who share our commitment to academic excellence, character development, and holistic growth. Our admission process is designed to be straightforward and supportive, ensuring every child has the opportunity to thrive.

Admission Process

1

Inquiry

Contact us or visit our school to learn about our programs, facilities, and admission requirements.

2

Application

Complete the admission application form and submit it along with the required documents.

3

Assessment

Your child will take a placement assessment to determine their academic level and readiness.

4

Enrollment

Upon successful assessment, complete the enrollment process and welcome your child to NGPS!

Requirements

Documents Needed

  • Completed application form
  • Birth certificate
  • Previous school reports (if applicable)
  • Passport-sized photographs
  • Parent/Guardian ID

Key Dates

  • Application opens: January 15
  • Application deadline: March 31
  • Placement assessment: April 1-15
  • Results announced: April 30
  • School begins: September 2

Fees

  • Application fee: GH 50
  • Tuition varies by grade level
  • Uniform and materials fee
  • Payment plans available
  • Scholarships for qualified students
